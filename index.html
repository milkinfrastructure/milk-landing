Welcome to Milk Infrastructure. Milk Parlor is the gateway. Keep the official OpenAI SDK and change only: OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://parlor.milkinfrastructure.com/v1 OPENAI_API_KEY=<your operator-issued Milk key> Existing Responses and Chat Completions calls stay the same. After each eligible completed answer, Parlor copies the request and response to your S3-compatible object storage. Milk Man watches those records, writes summary checkpoints, creates evals and training data, trains and compares a smaller model, and prepares an unsigned route for you to approve. No separate Milk SDK. No database. No standing GPU.
